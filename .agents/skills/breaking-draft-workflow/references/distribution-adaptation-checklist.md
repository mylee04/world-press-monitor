# Distribution Adaptation Checklist

## Keep constants

- Preserve core facts and source attribution.
- Keep timestamps and geography consistent across channels.

## Channel adaptation

- X/Twitter: short, high-signal, one core claim.
- Instagram: hook + context + concise takeaway.
- LinkedIn: context-first and professional tone.
- Newsletter: summary + why it matters + source links.

## Final validation

- Ensure no channel variant introduces new unsupported claims.
- Ensure all variants remain editable by journalist.
