# Breaking Editorial Checklist

## Signal quality

- At least one trusted source, preferably multiple confirmations.
- Publication time is recent enough for breaking context.

## Draft integrity

- Headline and body align on the same core claim.
- Timeline is explicit (what happened, when, by whom).
- Claims are attributable to listed sources.

## Language quality

- Spanish is clear, concise, and newsroom-neutral.
- Avoid exaggeration and certainty inflation.

## Safety

- No fabricated details.
- Mark unverified details as unconfirmed.
