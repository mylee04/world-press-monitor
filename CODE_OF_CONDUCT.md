# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

We expect respectful, constructive, and professional communication. We do not
tolerate harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that do not align with this
standard and may ban repeat offenders from participation.

For the full details, use: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
